Capacity Atlas

Three tools for deciding when a business is out of capacity and needs to sell, hold, hire, or add support — one for product/manufacturing, one for services, and a lightweight growth-stage version.

▛
Project 1 · Deterministic

Product & Manufacturing

You control the schedule. Jobs have known start dates and durations; capacity is billable hours by skill. Sell / Hold / Hire with cash, backlog, and concentration.
§
Project 2 · Probabilistic

Service Businesses

You don't control the timeline. Cases move through stages with uncertain external waits; three constraints (caseload, surge, hours) and a P90 band. Generalized from tax resolution.
≡
Project 3 · Spreadsheet

Capacity Atlas Lite

Growth-stage Excel suite: revenue-based capacity, org & hiring triggers, profitability of a new comp model, cash timing, and a comp optimizer.

Each project is self-contained. See the README in each folder for the model and core functions.
