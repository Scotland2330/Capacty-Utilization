How to use this report

Capacity Atlas tells any business — product, service, or nonprofit — when to sell more, hold, or add capacity, and what it means for cash and profit. Four steps:

1
Pick your business typeUse the switch up top — Product, Service, or Nonprofit. Each reads capacity its own way.
2
Enter or connect your dataType into the Assumptions panels in each section — or connect a source in Integrations to sync it automatically.
3
Read top to bottomStart with the Executive Summary, then cash flow, then the forecast. Each section says what it tells you.
4
ExportUse Export PDF for a board-ready report or Export CSV for the raw data.
What each section tells you
01 · Executive Summary
The headline. Are you over or under capacity, your recurring revenue and margin, your cash low point, and when you'll need to hire.
02 · 13-Week Cash Flow
"Will we make payroll?" Cash in vs. out every week and your running balance. Watch the trough — the lowest the balance gets.
03 · Financial Forecast
Where the P&L heads over 12 months as recurring revenue compounds — revenue, costs, and the bottom line.
04 · Capacity & Demand
The operating detail behind the verdict: capacity vs. booked + likely work each month, with the sell/hold/hire call.
05 · Data & Integrations
Where the numbers come from. Today it's demo data — connect your systems here to go live.
